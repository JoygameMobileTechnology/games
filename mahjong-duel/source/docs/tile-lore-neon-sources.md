# Neon Shrine: symbolism and context

Neon Shrine is fictional. Its traditional objects retain researched cultural context; meanings assigned to futuristic gadgets, snacks, count arrangements and roof levels are original readings for this collection. No magical properties or numeric meanings are claimed as historical fact. The images are new game artwork, not reproductions of the cited objects.

## Original interpretations

The lanterns express welcome and community; roof levels express continuity without assigning sacred meaning to their numbers. Geta evoke roots. Taiko, miniature shrine, umbrella, teacup, controller, memory chip, battery, cable, comet, telephone, food, hoverboard, wave, wireless signal, lock, heart, star, camera and pinwheel express values within the fictional setting. These readings are not presented as a dictionary of established Japanese religious symbols.
