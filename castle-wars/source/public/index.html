A VERY SHORT FIELD MANUAL

Make an entrance.

  1. Pull back. Let it fly.On your turn, drag backwards from the glowing shooter. Short pulls make softer shots; longer pulls add power. The dots show the start of your shot. Release to fire. You have 20 seconds.
  2. Break their heart. Or their crew.Destroy the golden core, or eliminate all three shooters. Low walls are tougher, but removing supports brings the castle down.
  3. Save the good stuff.Each completed round unlocks the same new weapon for both teams. Ammo stays until you use it. Construction tools use your turn too. All construction kits float without support. Build inside your original castle outline, including up to two bricks above its highest roof.
  4. Read the fortress and wind.Each match uses a pre-designed mirrored formation. Shooters behind cover need a higher arc. The arrow shows wind direction and strength. Wind bends every rocket and changes after one turn per player. Both shots share the same wind.
  5. Mind the rising tide.After four minutes, water rises after each of six final turns. Shooters drown at head height. Both last crews drowning together is a draw.

Keyboard: ← / → aim · ↑ / ↓ power · hold Space then release to fire · Esc cancels. Choose weapons with their buttons. Construction: arrows move the ghost; release Space to place.
