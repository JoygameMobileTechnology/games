HOW TO PLAY

90 seconds. Two mazes. Most dots wins.

Swipe (or WASD/arrows) to steer. Eat pips. Watch your opponent in the corner window.

CRATES — walking over one instantly fires an attack at your opponent. White = common, blue = uncommon, gold = rare.

REFLECT — super pellets grant one charge. Tap Reflect during the incoming warning to bounce an attack back. Siphon can ONLY be reflected.

DASH — 3 per match, short burst of speed. Escape, contest, close out.

Getting caught drops 10 of your dots on the floor — run back and reclaim them before they fade.

Dot waves at 0:30 and 0:60 respawn the dots furthest from you. Final 15 seconds: crates come faster.
